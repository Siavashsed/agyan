03.

زبان‌ها

کردیِ سورانی، فارسی، عربی، انگلیسی.

Kurdish - mother tongue

"Sorani is the language of the kitchen, of grief, of the lullaby. When I teach, I teach in Persian or English; but when I dream, I dream in Sorani. The academy keeps a Kurdish voice in everything it makes." Avin.

04.

پنج اصلِ کاری

  1. PR-01

    Ritual before content

    Before any technique, a moment of thanks. Without this, every method is mere exercise.

  2. PR-02

    The body knows first

    If a question does not answer, ask it in the belly, not in the head. The viscera have older intelligence.

  3. PR-03

    The ancestors are witnesses

    Women before you walked this road. In the silence, remember their names. Practice is never solitary.

  4. PR-04

    Manifestation requires gravity

    Desire without action is only smoke. The cosmos answers warm hands, not raised faces.

  5. PR-05

    Silence is a datum

    When no answer comes, the not-coming is the answer. Wait. The slowness of arrival is part of what arrives.
