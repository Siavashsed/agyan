۰۳. کتابخانه

کتابخانه

بیست و چهار کتاب در پنج طبقه.

01. تمرین صوفی

Mystical Dimensions of Islam
Annemarie Schimmel · 1975
REF / SUFI-01
Still the most useful single-volume introduction to Sufi history and thought in English. Read chapters 6 and 7 for the technical vocabulary of zikr and sama.
The Way of the Sufi
Idries Shah · 1968
REF / SUFI-02
Contested in academic circles, indispensable in working ones. Read the teaching-stories before the prefaces.
The Light of Faith and the Search for Truth
Sheikh Muzaffer Ozak · 1988
REF / SUFI-03
A Halveti-Jerrahi teacher writing in plain Turkish-inflected English. The chapter on breath and dhikr underlies our M-03.
Discourses of Rumi (Fihi Ma Fihi)
Jalal al-Din Rumi · A.J. Arberry tr. · 1961
REF / SUFI-04
Not the Masnavi. The kitchen Rumi, in talking voice. For students who have read too much Coleman Barks.

02. روان‌شناسیِ ژرف

Memories, Dreams, Reflections
C. G. Jung · 1962
REF / PSY-01
Begin here. Jung in his own first-person voice, ghostwritten with Aniela Jaffe. Read the chapter on Bollingen twice.
The Soul's Code
James Hillman · 1996
REF / PSY-02
The acorn theory of character. Listed under astrology background in our AS-01.
On Divination and Synchronicity
Marie-Louise von Franz · 1980
REF / PSY-03
A working scholar's sober reading of phenomena that most disciplines refuse to name. For MA-08.
Women Who Run With the Wolves
Clarissa Pinkola Estes · 1992
REF / PSY-04
Folktale and depth psychology, read together. The Bluebeard chapter is required reading before any forty-day vow.
Re-Visioning Psychology
James Hillman · 1975
REF / PSY-05
The book that announced the archetypal turn. Difficult prose; read twice, slowly.

03. تاریخِ کیهان‌نگاری

Ancient Astrology in Theory and Practice
Demetra George · 2019
REF / AST-01
A two-volume rigorous treatment of Hellenistic astrology in its own terms. The vocabulary appendix is worth the price.
Saturn: A New Look at an Old Devil
Liz Greene · 1976
REF / AST-02
Still the most psychologically literate book on a single planet. Read before AS-05.
Cosmos and Psyche
Richard Tarnas · 2006
REF / AST-03
Ambitious archetypal reading of outer-planet transits against modern history. Take with salt; take seriously.
The Stars Down to Earth
Theodor Adorno · 1957 / 1994
REF / AST-04
The critique of newspaper astrology, sharper now than then. We include it for honesty. The chart is not a forecast.
Tetrabiblos
Ptolemy · 2nd century CE · F.E. Robbins tr. · 1940
REF / AST-05
The classical source. Read for prose-quality and historical scope, not for technique.

04. سوماتیکس

The Body Keeps the Score
Bessel van der Kolk · 2014
REF / SOM-01
The trauma literature's standard. Read the chapters on yoga and IFS with care; skim the EMDR chapter.
In an Unspoken Voice
Peter Levine · 2010
REF / SOM-02
The somatic experiencing canon. Background for our M-04 (postural geometry).
Wisdom of the Body Moving
Linda Hartley · 1995
REF / SOM-03
Body-Mind Centering as a method. For students whose practice has become too cerebral.
Body and Earth
Andrea Olsen · 2002
REF / SOM-04
Anatomy and ecology, taught together. The chapter on the spine is especially good.

05. تجلی به مثابهٔ دیسیپلین

The War of Art
Steven Pressfield · 2002
REF / MAN-01
The clearest naming of internal resistance in modern English. Read before MA-07.
Atomic Habits
James Clear · 2018
REF / MAN-02
A toolkit for MA-06. Use the methods; ignore the success-mythology framing.
The Soul of Money
Lynne Twist · 2003
REF / MAN-03
An ethics of asking that does not flatten into hustle-talk. For MA-10.
Braiding Sweetgrass
Robin Wall Kimmerer · 2013
REF / MAN-04
Indigenous reciprocity as a model for desire that does not extract. Closes our AF track.
The Practice of Adaptive Leadership
Heifetz, Linsky, Grashow · 2009
REF / MAN-05
Organisational frame, but useful for the question: what change requires which act? Background for MA-04.
Bird by Bird
Anne Lamott · 1994
REF / MAN-06
A book on writing, smuggled in here. The single best treatment of beginning when you do not believe in your project.
"A library is not a list of books. It is a sequence of arguments the books have with one another, in the order in which you happen to read them." From Avin's commonplace book, undated.
