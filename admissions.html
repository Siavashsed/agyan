این فرم یک خبرنامه نیست.

Each year we admit between twelve and twenty students. The cohort runs from late September to mid June. Tuition is on a sliding scale; we do not turn away applicants for financial reasons. There is no application fee.

Please do not write a CV. Write the answer to the last field below as honestly as you can. That is what we read.

  • CYCLE
    One year, late September to mid June
  • COHORT
    12 to 20 students
  • FEES
    Sliding scale, by conversation
  • FORMAT
    Asynchronous reading, monthly live sessions, two weekend retreats

On not being a newsletter

The academy does not run an automated newsletter. We do not sell to a list. The monthly letter that goes out to enrolled students is written by hand, on a quiet day. If you want to read it, ask to be sent the last issue, and we will reply.
