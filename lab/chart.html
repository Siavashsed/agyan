01.

What the wheel shows

The outer ring carries the twelve zodiac signs, their glyphs visible at the perimeter. The middle ring marks the twelve houses, numbered counter-clockwise from the position of the Ascendant. The inner field carries the three luminaries: Sun (rose), Moon (lavender), Ascendant (heritage gold).

If you have not entered a time of birth, the Ascendant is omitted. Without time, no chart can claim accuracy at the level of houses. This is acknowledged everywhere in traditional practice.

02.

What it does not show

This is a visual sketch, not an ephemeris calculator. Sun position is approximated from day-of-year. Moon is a deterministic pseudo-random placement; the real Moon moves through a sign every two and a half days. Ascendant is a rough two-hour-per-sign window starting at 6am.

For a chart you intend to study, use a proper ephemeris tool. We use this widget for the felt sense of the wheel itself, which the AS track returns to repeatedly.
