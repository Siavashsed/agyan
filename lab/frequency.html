The Earth tone
CLASSICAL / EQUILIBRIUM
Volume50%
Binaural
A natural reference. Older instruments, choirs and tuning forks were often pitched here. Many find it soft in the body in a way 440 Hz is not. Useful as a baseline before exploring the rest.
For binaural mode, wear headphones - the offset between left and right channels is what creates the beat. Without headphones it sounds like a single tone, which still works as a focus signal.
This tool is reflective, not prescriptive. Lower the volume before pressing play.
