زبان نقشهٔ زایچه

Astrology, in this academy, is not prediction. It is a literacy: a vocabulary for reading personal structure against a cosmic alphabet that has been in use since Babylon. We treat the natal chart the way a literature student treats a sonnet. The chart is the text; the practice is the close reading.

This is not divination. We do not foretell. We use the chart as a structured mirror, a scaffold for self-knowledge that is older than any therapy and stranger than any horoscope column.

Lessons AS-01 through AS-03 are open. The remaining nine require admission.

04.

بستن این شاخه

By the end of the year, the student should be able to spend ninety quiet minutes alone with a chart and emerge with five concrete sentences about a life. Not predictions. Sentences. The discipline is the sentence, not the certainty.

"The chart is the only document I read that I did not write." Avin, in conversation with a third-cohort student, 2018.
  1. The academy works mostly with the tropical zodiac, the placidus house system, and a Hellenistic-Jungian hybrid interpretive frame. Other systems are introduced for comparison.
  2. For students enrolled in the programme, lesson AS-10 includes a video of Avin reading her own chart aloud.
  3. The chart wheel in the Lab is a visual sketch only. For an astronomically precise chart, students should use a dedicated ephemeris tool.
