The shape of a year

Each track is twelve lessons. Together they form a single one-year programme, with overlapping rhythms. A typical student arrives in autumn, sits in meditation through winter, takes up the natal chart at the spring equinox, walks manifestation through summer, and closes the year with affirmation at first frost. The order is not mandatory; it is a suggestion built from twelve cohorts of experience.

On open access

Lessons 01, 02, and 03 of every track are open to all visitors. They are written to stand alone. If you find what you need there, that is enough; we are not interested in your enrollment for its own sake. The full twelve are for those who wish to walk the road in company.
