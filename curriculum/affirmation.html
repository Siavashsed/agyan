AF / آیین کلام

نیت‌گویی و تکرار

Affirmation, as we use the word in the academy, is the practice of language as ritual. It belongs to the same family as the Sufi zikr, the Vedic mantra, and the Christian monologistos: short sentences repeated until the body accepts them as a kind of architecture.

The popular self-help industry has reduced this practice to the mirror and the sticky note. We work it differently. The twelve lessons here treat the affirmative sentence as a piece of sonic engineering, with attention to vowel, repetition rate, posture, time of day, and the difference between speech that the speaker believes and speech that she does not yet believe.

Lessons AF-01 through AF-03 are open. The remaining nine are reserved for students in the one-year programme.

03.

آهنگِ تمرین

هفته

One sentence, every morning

The same sentence, six days a week, ninety-nine times. Aloud or whispered.

ماه

Handwritten transcript

At the end of each month, write the month's sentence in long-hand, three times slowly.

سه‌ماه

The forty-day vow

Once a season. A single sentence, forty consecutive mornings, no exception.

04.

بستن این شاخه

By the end of the year, the student will have spoken at least a thousand morning affirmations and walked through one forty-day vow. The signs of completion are quieter than the signs of beginning. The mouth holds the sentence even when it is not speaking.

"An affirmation is a small sentence with which the speaker negotiates with the dead, the future, and her own body, in that order." Avin, lecture notes, 2019.
  1. The phrase "ritual ecology" is borrowed from the anthropologist ; we use it loosely to mean the entire surround of a repeated practice, not just the central act.
  2. The affirmation generator in the Lab is a working aid, not a substitute for finding one's own sentence.
RHYTHM OF PRACTICE

Suggested cadence

DAILY

A single sentence, spoken aloud once on waking and once before sleep.

WEEKLY

Review the week's sentence on Sunday. Edit one word if it has stopped landing.

QUARTERLY

New sentence at each season change. Keep the old ones in a notebook.
